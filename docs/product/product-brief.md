# Product Brief

## Product

PetCura is the WhatsApp-native ClientOps inbox for veterinary clinics.

It captures owner requests, structures intake, suggests category and risk flags, helps staff reply faster with reviewed AI context, manages reminders, and exports clean case records into the clinic's existing PMS.

## Wedge

Reduce routine phone interruptions and missed follow-ups in 30 days without replacing the clinic PMS or forcing owners to download an app.

## Customer

Independent or small-chain EU veterinary clinics with 2-10 vets, high phone or WhatsApp volume, and no strong digital front-office system.

## V1 Scope

- WhatsApp intake
- AI-assisted clinic-owned web intake on `/intake`
- clinic inbox
- request detail
- staff-approved replies
- AI intake questions, summaries, translations, category suggestions, risk flags, routing suggestions, and reply drafts
- reviewed AI memory for request, pet, and owner context
- staff-confirmed urgency
- reminders
- PDF/CSV export
- audit logs and AI logs
- route-only service intents for grooming, delivery, walking, and boarding when a clinic wants those requests in its inbox

## V1 Non-Goals

- native mobile apps
- marketplace, commerce, delivery operations, logistics, payments
- insurance, external grooming marketplace, pet sitters, walking operations, boarding operations
- diagnosis, prescription, or autonomous medical advice
- staff, clinic-wide, or cross-clinic AI memory
- full PMS replacement
- public API
- multi-region expansion outside the EU
